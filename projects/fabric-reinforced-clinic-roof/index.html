Rock Springs, Wyoming / October 2025

Fabric-reinforced clinic roof

We restored this clinic roof with polyester fabric at each seam and across the full surface.

Seam reinforcement

The specification called for pressure-washing and priming the roof, then embedding four-inch fabric strips in base coat at each seam.

Full-surface reinforcement

A second layer of fabric covered the roof. Around A/C units and other openings, the system used fabric and sealant to join the edges to the roof. A reflective white topcoat covered the reinforced surface.
