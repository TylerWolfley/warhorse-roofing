Metal roof restoration

Metal panels can still be serviceable when their laps or fasteners start letting water through. We check the panels, connections, rust, and previous repairs before recommending restoration.

On a suitable roof, we clean the metal, address corrosion, and use a rust-inhibiting primer. The seams are reinforced and fastener heads sealed before the reflective coating goes over the panels.

Fabric-reinforced roofing

On a suitable low-slope roof, polyester fabric goes into the wet base coat. It reinforces the surface where the roof meets a wall, turns around equipment, or crosses a seam. The finish coat covers that reinforced layer.

On a clinic roof in Rock Springs, the specification called for fabric strips along each seam and another layer of fabric across the roof.

Before you decide

Talk to Wade
Can my roof be restored?

We look at the existing material, how it drains, and whether old repairs have held. A leak around one curb may call for a local repair. Widespread trapped moisture, loose material, or a damaged deck can mean sections need replacement before a coating is an option.

What goes into the estimate?

Wade looks at the areas that need repair, the preparation for your roof surface, and the system that would go over it. Ask him to walk you through the scope around seams, walls, and equipment, as well as access and scheduling.

Can my roof qualify for a 20-year warranty?

A limited manufacturer’s warranty of up to 20 years may be available for qualifying installations. The manufacturer must approve the application. Wade can explain the term, coverage, and maintenance requirements for the system proposed for your building.

Can the building stay open?

That depends on access and the work overhead. Tell us which doors, loading bays, and rooftop equipment your business needs during the day so we can plan the work with you.

Have a roof you want us to look at?

Tell Wade where the building is and what you’ve noticed.
